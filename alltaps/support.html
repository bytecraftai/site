Try it: tap to add, minus to undo

Wait, that's it? Yep. On purpose.

AllTaps is small, fast and stays out of your way. It lives on your wrist. Not on a dashboard you'll never look at, not in a CRM of your shots, not in a feed asking how you felt about hole 7. We obsessed over what to leave out as much as what to put in.

What we left out, and why

  • No GPS, yardage or hole maps. Other apps do that beautifully. This isn't that.
  • No swing analysis. We don't have an opinion about your swing.
  • No subscriptions, signup or premium tier. The app you download is the app you get.
  • No leaderboards, social feed or friends list. Your league has a group text. Use that.
  • No notifications. You'll never get a push from AllTaps telling you to come back.
  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking. We don't know who you are, and we'd like to keep it that way.

What we obsessed over instead

  • One tap, one shot. That's the whole interaction model.
  • Big buttons. Watch screens are tiny. Thumbs aren't. We designed for thumbs.
  • Saves automatically, resumes anywhere. Watch dies, sun in your eyes, lost track mid-round: AllTaps remembers.
  • Tiny app, tiny battery footprint. No background syncing, no pinging the cloud. Open it, count, close it.
  • Shareable scorecards. If the app's job is over in 30 seconds, the least we can do is leave you something fun to text your friends.

If you want a Swiss Army knife, the App Store is full of them. We made a tee. ⛳

Common questions.

Can't find your answer? Email hello@bytecraftco.ai.

How do I save my round?

AllTaps saves automatically after every shot. There's also a Save & Exit button at the bottom of the round screen if you want to leave the app and come back later. Your progress will be waiting on the home screen as Resume Round.

I tapped the counter too many times. Can I undo a shot?

Yes. Tap the coral − button below the counter to remove the last shot from the current hole. You can also use the back arrow to revisit a previous hole and edit its score.

How do I share my scorecard?

After you finish a round, tap Share Scorecard on the scorecard screen. AllTaps renders a scorecard image and hands it to Apple's share sheet, where you can send it by Messages or Mail. Your Apple Watch needs to be paired and connected to your iPhone for the share to complete.

Where are my past rounds?

From the home screen, tap Previous Rounds. The button appears after you've completed at least one round. Tap any round to see its scorecard again or share the image.

How do I delete a round or all my data?

For now, the simplest way is to uninstall AllTaps. Every saved round is erased instantly. There's nothing in the cloud to clean up because nothing was ever sent there.

Deleting a single round is on our list for a future update.

Why doesn't AllTaps have GPS, yardage, hole maps or par tracking?

By design. AllTaps is built to be the simplest possible counter: perfect for casual rounds, league nights, and anyone who finds full-featured golf apps to be overkill. If you want GPS and shot-by-shot analytics, lots of great apps do that. We do one thing: count.

Does AllTaps need an internet connection?

No. Once installed, AllTaps works completely offline. The only feature that touches the network is Share Scorecard, and that just hands off to Apple's own share sheet.

Is my data private?

Yes. AllTaps collects nothing. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Will AllTaps come to iPhone or iPad?

Maybe. The Apple Watch app is the priority. If there's enough demand for an iPhone companion, we'll consider it.
