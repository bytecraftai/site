AllTaps

Tap. Score. Share.

The simplest golf scorecard for Apple Watch. Tap each shot, advance each hole, and finish with a beautiful scorecard you can share with yourself or your friends.

Free. Apple Watch Series 7 or later (watchOS 10 or later).

Three taps to a scorecard.

No accounts to create, no settings to configure. Open it on your wrist and play.

What it does.

Everything you need to keep score, and nothing you don't.

One tap per shotA big counter with custom haptics.
9 or 18 holesPick your round length when you start.
Name the courseSpeak the course name with dictation, or type it.
Save and resumeSave and exit mid-round, then resume right where you left off.
Illustrated scorecardsA beautiful illustrated scorecard for every round.
Share in a tapSend it by Messages or Mail.

How to install on your Apple Watch

Easiest: open the App Store right on your watch and search "AllTaps." Tap Get.

Or from your iPhone: tap Download above. If it doesn't appear on your watch within a minute, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps, then tap Install next to AllTaps.

Private by design.

AllTaps collects nothing, sends nothing, tracks nothing. Every round stays on your wrist: no servers, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
