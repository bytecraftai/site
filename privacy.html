Who we are

This notice covers the website bytecraftco.ai, run by BYTECRAFTCO LLC. The Modern Recovery is a dba of BYTECRAFTCO LLC and has its own privacy policy on themodernrecovery.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, for example the AllTaps privacy policy.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms:

  • Partner with the lab form: your name, company or brand, work email, website (if you add one), the type of enquiry, the category (if you choose one) and your message.
  • Early access form: your email address.

If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

How we use it

We use what you send only to reply to your enquiry or, for the early access list, to tell you when the app launches. We never sell your details and never share them with anyone else for their own use.

Form service

Our forms are delivered by a third-party form service, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). When you press send, it receives your submission and forwards it to our inbox: partner enquiries go to hello@themodernrecovery.com and early access sign-ups go to hello@bytecraftco.ai. FormSubmit handles the submission under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions in our email for as long as we need them to handle your enquiry or the early access list, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, or to leave the early access list, email hello@bytecraftco.ai. We will act on your request and confirm by email.

This website

bytecraftco.ai does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. Like most websites, it loads fonts from Google Fonts and an animation library from cdnjs, so those providers receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads.

Contact

Questions about this notice: hello@bytecraftco.ai. If we change it, we will update the date at the top of this page.
